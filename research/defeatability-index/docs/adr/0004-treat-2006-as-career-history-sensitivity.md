# Treat 2006 as a career-history sensitivity

Status: accepted

The candidate-history study uses 2010, 2014, and 2022 stable-boundary general elections as
its primary leave-one-election-out evaluation and reports 2006 separately. The source dataset
begins in 2003, leaving 2006 candidates only three years of observable career history: just two
of 275 candidates have an observed earlier other-office run and none has an observed earlier
win. A pooled held-out improvement, contest-clustered interval, and contest-aware permutation
test determine support; election-level direction is a heterogeneity diagnostic rather than a
unanimity requirement. This avoids letting left-censored feature measurement veto the study,
at the cost of a primary evaluation with only three election holdouts and a protocol revision
made after inspecting the 2006 coverage pattern.
