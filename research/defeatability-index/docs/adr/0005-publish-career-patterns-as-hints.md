# Publish career patterns as hints, not a score

Status: accepted

The 2026 site will expose supported career-history patterns as candidate-specific Historical
hints triggered by the candidate's own record, Candidate regime, and Opponent field. These
hints do not enter the Defeatability score or a general winner model: each carries its own
historical comparison, sample size, and uncertainty, and uses associational rather than causal
language. This preserves interpretable evidence for readers at the cost of maintaining a
separate hint catalog and withholding model patterns that cannot support a standalone statement.
