# Model named-set ward vote shares with sparse historical evidence

The user wants readers to judge already-publicized ward polling in light of small samples and methodological weaknesses, and explicitly chose modelled ranges among named candidates rather than a descriptive historical-error span. Council race cards therefore publish a limited joint vote-share model conditional on exactly the candidates named in each poll. This is a narrow extension to ADR 0043: it does not establish a full-ballot Council Forecast, winner probabilities, or allocation of Other to omitted names.

For each historical contest, normalize source shares across its named candidates and normalize official outcomes across those same people. Model the result vector as Dirichlet(kappa × poll vector), with log(kappa) varying by ward around an uncertain population mean. The mean has Normal(log(30), 1.5) prior; between-ward standard deviation has HalfNormal(1) prior. These explicit regularizers are modelling assumptions, not respondent counts or historical facts. Integrate both hyperparameters and future ward concentration. Retain median and central 80% marginal ranges as audit outputs from joint unit-sum draws. The main public chart shows a histogram of the reported leader’s advantage over the strongest other named candidate in each draw. Mix the Dirichlet and logistic-normal scenario draws equally, with explicit percentage-point and scenario-frequency axes and a tie line. Equal weights express modelling judgment, not learned model probabilities; six same-cycle wards cannot reliably select mixture weights. The distribution includes error-scale and family uncertainty, and the separate model sensitivities remain in the audit. Do not feature precise-looking medians or numerical whole-ward winner chances. This directly addresses the user’s concern that a polished vote-share chart over-legitimizes the poll. Original toplines remain visible. All model quantities keep the named-set denominator explicit.

The six horizon-comparable 2022 Forum ward contests supply six joint observations, not 26 independent elections. Their discrepancy includes sampling, survey bias, response mapping and subsequent movement; six polls cannot identify these separately. Carry that total discrepancy into the current mixed-mode polls without sample-size shrinkage, since effective sizes and a mode/cycle adjustment are unidentifiable here. Transfer across cycles and modes remains an explicit limitation, not an estimated correction.

Preserve exact original percentages, field, source links and question-level bases separately. Do not map published Other to the eventual unreported vote: historical full-field polls themselves sometimes published Other. Every displayed model states the named-set denominator; unreported candidates receive no individual estimate. Earlier incompatible/hypothetical fields retain their original descriptive display.

Use actual leave-one-contest-out refits, marginal interval scores and explicit failures. Publish prior and logistic-normal distribution sensitivities as audit metadata; do not claim calibration from six same-cycle tests. Numerical gates check quadrature convergence, integration-grid tails and replicated predictive quantiles, distinct from empirical model evaluation. This is a static current-stage model; a Kalman/time-series filter is unjustified by the sparse repeated ward readings. See the retained source audit and model-validation note for exact evidence and assumptions.
