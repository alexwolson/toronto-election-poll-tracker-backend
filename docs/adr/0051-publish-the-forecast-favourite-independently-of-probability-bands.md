# Publish the Forecast Favourite independently of Probability Bands

The Band Stability Gate answers whether every Mandatory Sensitivity Variant supports one public Probability Band; crossing a band boundary can withhold that band even when every variant agrees on which candidate leads. The feed will therefore publish a separate Forecast Favourite when every applicable candidate-win tier gate is open, every Mandatory Sensitivity Variant identifies the same unique candidate with the highest Candidate Win Probability, and that candidate's 95% numerical-error interval lies strictly above every rival's interval in every variant. This categorical claim does not publish or relax any probability estimate: Candidate Win Probability bands retain their existing independent gates and remain unavailable when their band stability fails.

This keeps all eligible Poll Readings in the Internal Outcome Distribution and distinguishes uncertainty about precision from uncertainty about ordering. For the September 2026 five-reading forecast, the polling-only bridge places Chow at 86.90% and the incumbency sensitivity moves her above 90%; both identify Chow as the separated favourite even though they straddle the coarse 90% Probability Band boundary. The rejected alternatives were to discard an eligible reading to recover the earlier bands, to infer a favourite in the frontend from whichever candidate cards remained visible, or to publish the bridge's band despite sensitivity disagreement.
