# Publish the uncertainty ladder beneath the forecast

The mayoral forecast feed gains one additive block, `uncertainty`, and the homepage gains one strip beneath the win probabilities that shows where the forecast's doubt comes from. The headline forecast, the three-outcome margin view, the vote ranges and the win probabilities are unchanged; schema 4 and the policy `margin-first-joint-draws-v1` are unchanged.

**Why.** Under ADR 0055 the poll leader's win probability is about 70% while the polls put her 10 points ahead. Readers, and the maintainer, asked why the number is not higher. The answer is not one thing, and the model already holds it: every simulation passes through three states. What the polls say now, with only sampling and house-effect noise; support on election day after the rest of the campaign's movement; and the result, after the election-day error learned from seven past races. Read in that order, which is the order things happen, the challenger's chance of finishing ahead rises from about 5% to about 17% to about 29% on the 2026-09-22 fit, and the middle of the margin barely moves while its range widens. Publishing the three snapshots lets the reader see that the forecast's uncertainty is mostly about what has not happened yet, and how much of it is each source.

**What is published.** `uncertainty` names the same leader and challenger as `pairwise_margin` and carries three `steps` keyed `polls_today`, `campaign_movement` and `election_day`, each with the leader-minus-challenger gap in full-ballot vote-share points (median and central 80% interval) and the probability that each candidate is ahead at that snapshot. Every step is computed from the same joint draws as everything else on the page (the `current`, `election_support` and `named_result` sites, scaled by the residual pool so the units match), and the `election_day` step reproduces the published `pairwise_margin` exactly. A withdrawal by a named candidate is not modelled and is not shown; the model has no mechanism for it and the corpus has one two-month-old paired question on the subject, which is not a basis for a published number.

**Presentation.** Three rows on one margin axis with the tie marked; each row's bar is the 80% range of the gap at that snapshot, tinted by which candidate is ahead, with the median as a tick and the challenger's chance of being ahead at the right. Chosen on 2026-09-22 from four sketches because it shows uncertainty as a range that opens rather than as slices of one candidate's number. The third source is labelled as results landing away from final polls, not as polling error, because it includes late change the polls could not see; contributions are order-dependent and the model's generative order is the one used.

**Consequences.** The block is additive: a frontend built for schema 4 without it still validates and renders; the frontend that renders it treats a missing block as "do not show" and a malformed one as invalid. No model change, no re-qualification, no pre-registration needed. First release carrying the block: the Backend release after `backend-2026-09-22.1`.
