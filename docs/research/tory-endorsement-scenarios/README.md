## Reading the scenarios

A positive effect transfers support to the endorsed candidate. A negative effect reverses the specified flows. Net realized effect equals potential effect multiplied by the selected retained fraction. That fraction includes persistence and any restriction from already-cast ballots; it is an assumption, not an estimated exposure or turnout rate. Dates alone never change the result. The 2023 episode does not identify retention five or 47 days before the election.

The reference holds dated poll shares and every other campaign change fixed. It is neither an election-day baseline forecast nor an identified no-endorsement counterfactual. No causal effect, confidence interval, or win probability is fitted. Do not add the shifts to post-event polls that may already reflect an endorsement.

The 2023 first-post-event comparisons were +5 points (Liaison) and +7 (Forum). Existing momentum and the same-day Star endorsement prevent isolating Tory's contribution. See [the evidence and modeling memo](../john-tory-2023-endorsement-evidence.md) for sources and limits.

The [pretrend and council comparison analysis](../endorsement-identification/README.md) calculates 3.6–4.7 points above Bailão's preceding two to three weeks of momentum, or 5.2–6.5 points using four weeks. These are joint late-campaign residuals, not a Tory coefficient. The council audit does not yet supply a causal Star effect to subtract.

Verification: every emitted feasible scenario conserves 100 points and has no negative support; infeasible scenarios expose an explanation instead of a result. Canonical readings and forecast feeds are read-only. No model refresh was run.

## Challenger consolidation

The [consolidation analysis](../tory-consolidation-dynamics.md) separates Bailão's share of a selected candidate pool from growth of that pool. The source-linked historical transcription and its SHA-256 are included in JSON. These descriptive ratios do not identify voters' ideology, individual switches or Tory's causal contribution.

Another 404 scenarios transfer 0–100% (one-percent steps) of the other challenger's support to the recipient, for two baselines and two recipients. This fraction is a chosen net realized transfer, independent of the point-shift and timing settings. Chow and the residual stay fixed; Bradford plus Alexander is conserved. A common slider permits comparison, not an assumption that the two endorsements are equally effective. The CSV remains the original 360-row point grid; all consolidation inputs and results are in JSON.
