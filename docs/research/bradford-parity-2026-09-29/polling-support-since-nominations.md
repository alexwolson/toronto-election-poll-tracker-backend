# Polling support since nominations closed

A standalone publication figure of the website's default **Polling support over time → Candidate choices → Since nominations closed** view, using the September 29, 2026 Polling release. The cutoff is August 21, 2026; fieldwork must end after that date. Six public samples report Chow, Bradford and Alexander, with fieldwork ending September 5–24.

`polling-trend-data.json` is the frozen extracted chart data: all 18 displayed candidate-choice points, full-history LOESS curves, firm labels, published versus derived shares, and the selected poll IDs. Its source was the September 29 release's `mayoral_polling.json`, with the same 25 archive polls as the Polling release. The extraction applies the frontend's candidate-choice conversion and post-nomination field filter, and executes the frontend's actual `src/lib/loess.ts` through TypeScript's transpiler. The LOESS fit uses the full comparable polling history (span 0.6, at least five distinct dates, 40 grid points); only its post-nomination portion is displayed. Ipsos September 8 was published among all respondents, so its reported choice categories were divided by the full expressed candidate-choice total before plotting. No additional three-name normalization was applied: reported Other candidate choices remain in the denominator. These are poll observations and a descriptive smoother, not forecast probabilities.

`figure_polling_support.py` renders the frozen extraction with Vega-Altair and vl-convert. Candidate fills, lines and shapes follow the website: Chow purple circle, Bradford green square and Alexander teal diamond. Exports are `polling-support-since-nominations.{png,pdf,svg,html,vl.json}`. The x-axis begins August 22, like the website's default view, and ends September 29 to show the September 24 labels. If extracting again, run `extract_polling_trend.cjs` from the frontend root with the Polling release feed path and the desired output JSON path; the exported figure itself needs only the frozen JSON and the figure requirements already listed in `requirements-figure.txt`.

Presentation revision: all three point symbols now have a white centre and a stroke in the candidate's colour, including Alexander's teal diamonds. Text uses a consistent regular weight. The symbol treatment is an intentional standalone-figure styling choice; the poll values and site-derived LOESS are unchanged.
