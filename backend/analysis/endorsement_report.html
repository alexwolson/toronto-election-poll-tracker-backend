What 2023 contributes

Ana Bailão rose 5 points in Liaison and 7 points in Forum in their first subsequent polls after Tory’s June 21 endorsement. She was already gaining, and the Star endorsed her the same day. This history supplies a scale to explore, not a measured Tory effect.

Continuing her preceding two to three weeks of momentum leaves 3.6–4.7 points above trend in those polls. Four-week trends leave 5.2–6.5 points. These residuals still combine Tory, the Star, other campaign changes and polling error.

Across 21 Star-endorsed council candidates in 2022, election-day support averaged 3.1 points below advance-vote support. This excludes Ward 23, where a candidate’s death changed the counted field. Different electorates and overlapping endorsements prevent treating the contrast as the Star’s effect. It cannot simply be subtracted from Bailão’s increase.

Calendar dates do not estimate retention. The selected percentage is an assumption about the net effect realized by October 26, including persistence and any inability to switch already-cast ballots. No turnout model or uncast-ballot adjustment is inferred.

The baseline is a dated poll, not an election-day prediction or a measured no-endorsement counterfactual.
